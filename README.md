# Spicetify Miniplayer Volume Scroll

A lightweight [Spicetify](https://spicetify.app/) extension that adds mouse-over scrolling volume control to Spotify's official Miniplayer.

Even when the Miniplayer is collapsed to it's smallest size where the volume slider disappears, hovering anywhere over the Miniplayer window and rolling your mouse scroll wheel will seamlessly adjust playback volume.

---

## Features

- **Mouse-Over Scrolling Volume:** Adjust playback volume by scrolling anywhere over the floating Miniplayer.
- **Smooth Increments:** Steps volume up or down in 5% increments per scroll tick.
